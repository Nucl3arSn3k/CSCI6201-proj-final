Dataset information

Dataset name:
311 City Service Requests in 2025

Source / organization:
Government of the District of Columbia (DC Open Data), 311 service request data.

URL:
https://opendata.dc.gov/datasets/85a2474b468b4dfeb84f9165210fe5a3_18/explore?location=38.909622%2C-77.023480%2C17

Description:
One row per 311 service request made in Washington, DC during 2025 (440,600
requests, 33 columns). Each record holds request type and category, the agency
that handled it, dates added and resolved, status, ward, street address, and
latitude/longitude. Used here to study sanitation-type requests and, later,
their relationship to bar locations.

Original or modified:
The data values are the original, unaltered download. The only change to the file
is that it was gzip-compressed (311_City_Service_Requests_in_2025.csv.gz).
